The latest update is an improvement, but I believe the platform is still being structured around assets rather than decisions. Current architecture still feels: DATABASE-FIRST Examples: * Indices * Metals * Forex * Bonds * Stocks * ETFs This is logical technically. But users do not think in asset classes. Users think: 1. What is happening? 2. Why does it matter? 3. What opportunities exist? 4. What does it mean for my portfolio? I want the platform to become: INTELLIGENCE-FIRST NOT: asset-list-first. --- NEW HIGH LEVEL STRUCTURE 1. MARKETS Purpose: Understand the environment. Questions answered: * Is this risk-on or risk-off? * Is liquidity improving? * What is the dollar doing? * What are yields doing? * Which sectors are leading? * What changed today? Core outputs: * Market Regime * Liquidity * Dollar * Rates * Sector Rotation * Macro Briefing * What Changed Today --- 2. INVESTMENTS Purpose: Find opportunities. Questions answered: * What deserves attention? * Which stocks are improving? * Where is momentum? * Where are earnings revisions improving? * What themes are strengthening? Core outputs: * Watchlists * Opportunity Feed * Relative Strength * Earnings Changes * Catalyst Tracking * Research --- 3. PORTFOLIO Purpose: Understand personal positioning. Questions answered: * Am I positioned correctly? * What is helping my portfolio? * What is hurting my portfolio? * What risks are increasing? * What should I review? Core outputs: * Portfolio Intelligence * Macro Alignment * Thesis Tracking * Concentration Risk * Portfolio Stress Testing * Daily Portfolio Briefing --- IMPORTANT Asset universes should remain available: * Stocks * ETFs * FX * Bonds * Commodities * Indices But these should become secondary navigation and data sources. They should NOT dominate the UX. The primary experience should surface: * intelligence * prioritisation * context * opportunities * and decision support rather than giant ticker universes and asset lists. I want the platform to feel like: "an intelligence layer on top of markets" NOT: "a database of market assets." Please redesign the information architecture, homepage hierarchy, and navigation around this principle.
